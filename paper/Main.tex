\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper introduced MPDenoiseNet (Multi-Path Denoise Net), a novel multi-path deep learning architecture for efficient and effective denoising across diverse noise types. By integrating specialized SeConv blocks for impulse noise, Anisotropic Diffusion for edge perserving smoothing, Transformers for global context, and a U-Net AutoEncoder for reconstruction, MPDenoiseNet leverages multiple strengths. Experimental results confirmed MPDenoiseNet's denoising is competitive with state-of-the-art methods like Restormer, while being significantly more resource efficient.
