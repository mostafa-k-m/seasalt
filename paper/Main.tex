\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this paper, we proposed MPDenoiseNet, a multi-pathway deep learning model for image denoising that effectively handles diverse noise types while remaining computationally efficient. By combining SeConv blocks, Anisotropic Diffusion blocks, Transformer blocks, and an asymmetric U-Net decoder, MPDenoiseNet achieves strong denoising performance. Experimental results show that MPDenoiseNet outperforms or matches the state-of-the-art Restormer in PSNR and SSIM across Gaussian, Poisson, Bernoulli, and Salt \& Pepper noise. Additionally, MPDenoiseNet demonstrates significantly lower memory usage and higher throughput, enabling it to process larger images. Ablation studies confirm the importance of each architectural component, especially Transformers and the U-Net. Overall, MPDenoiseNet offers a practical, high-performance solution for real-time anad resource-constrained denoising applications.
