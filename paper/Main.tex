\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper introduced MPDNet (Multi-Pathway Denoise Net), a novel multi-pathway deep learning architecture for efficient and effective denoising across diverse noise types. By integrating specialized SeConv blocks for impulse noise, Anisotropic Diffusion for edge perserving smoothing, Transformers for global context, and a U-Net AutoEncoder for reconstruction, MPDNet leverages multiple strengths. Experimental results confirmed MPDNet's denoising is competitive with state-of-the-art methods like Restormer, while being significantly more resource efficient.
